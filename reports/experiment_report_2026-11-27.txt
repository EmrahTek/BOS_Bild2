- Focus-ring marking: approximately 0.4 m; focus on the pattern.
- Reported camera-to-pattern distance: 47 cm; nominal dots: 0.80 mm.
- LED backlight and extra paper retained; no later change reported.

Correlation:
- Normalize RGB8 to grayscale in [0, 1].
- Normalized cross-correlation with parabolic subpixel peak refinement.
- Window: 40 x 40 pixels; grid step: 20 pixels; search radius: 4 pixels.
- Number of grid positions for these images: 4524.
- Peak minimum: 0.60; minimum separated-peak margin: 0.03 where finite.
- Minimum intensity standard deviation: 0.01.
- Search-boundary peaks and failed quality/interpolation checks are rejected.
- Invalid displacement values are NaN and must not be treated as zero.
- Displacement magnitude is sqrt(u^2 + v^2), in pixels.
- No image registration or global-motion subtraction was applied.

Pair acquisition:
- Each pair used its own cold reference followed by a heated measurement.
- About 30 seconds was suggested for candle warm-up, but actual heater-on times
  were not recorded. Reference-to-measurement gaps are not exact warm-up times.

Video acquisition/export:
- Each heated frame compared with a single cold reference for that sequence.
- Initial candle target: 10 s at 10 fps; achieved approximately 5.11 fps.
- Hairdryer target: 10 s at 5 fps; achieved approximately 5.01 fps.
- Rotate 90 degrees clockwise during processing.
- Retained hairdryer videos: horizontal component u, display range -2 to +2 px,
  export rate 5 fps, H.264/libx264, 1000 x 700 presentation frames.
- The candle export used u with a -1 to +1 px scale and 10 fps output.
- Real relative sample times are preserved by holding each measurement until
  the next one. Export fps does not create additional measurements.
- A duration slightly greater than 10 s rounds up to 51 output frames at 5 fps,
  giving a 10.2 s MP4. The raw observation interval remains about 10 s.

10. ASSESSMENT AND REMAINING LIMITATIONS

The setup produced repeatable qualitative candle-plume displacement structures
and short offline BOS videos. The selected practical acquisition setting was
f/4, 5 ms, gain 0, with a reported 47 cm camera-to-pattern distance and 0.80 mm
printed dots. This is a working setting, not a demonstrated optical optimum.

Quantitative temperature, density, and velocity values were not determined.
There was no ground-truth warm-air measurement, calibrated magnification,
confirmed heat-source plane distance, cold-blowing control, or uncertainty study
for the real experiments. Therefore a larger pixel shift must not be reported
as a higher temperature or a faster flow.

The repeated video sampling rate is about 5 measurements/s, limiting temporal
resolution. Spatial resolution is set by the 40-pixel interrogation windows and
20-pixel grid, not the full 1600 x 1200 pixel image. Reflections, obstruction,
illumination changes, and global motion remain possible contributors.

Recommended next evidence to record: the two intermediate geometry distances,
lens focal length/model, exact source position and operating mode, ambient
conditions, controlled wait times, and a cold-blowing hairdryer control.
